Support

Keep’em helps you clean up your photo gallery with a swipe: keep it or trash it, all on your phone. Questions, problems or ideas: tomerfer@gmail.com.

Cancel a subscription: in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions; on iPhone in Settings → your name → Subscriptions; or in the app: Settings → Manage subscription.

Restore a purchase on a new phone: Settings → Restore purchases, with the same Google or Apple account.

Deleting your data: everything the app stores stays on your phone. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its data) deletes it.

Support

MuchMeat shows per-kg meat prices at butcher shops and supermarket meat counters in Israel, on a map. The community reports prices so everyone can see where it's cheapest. Questions, problems or ideas: tomerfer@gmail.com.

Browsing the map and prices needs no account. An account (phone number) is only needed to report prices, give feedback, follow shops or get alerts.

Deleting your account: in the app, Profile → Delete account (מחיקת חשבון). No access to the app? See Delete account.

Developer: Tomer Fernandes. The privacy policy and terms are in Hebrew.
